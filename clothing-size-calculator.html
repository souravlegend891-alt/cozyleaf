How This Size Calculator Works

Each tab uses its own reference chart built from standard international measurement ranges — chest and bust circumference for tops, waist for bottoms, a combination of bust/waist/hip for dresses, and foot length for shoes. Enter your measurements in centimeters or inches, and the closest matching size highlights instantly in the table.

Why Brand Sizing Still Varies

No universal size chart perfectly predicts fit across every brand, because cut, fabric stretch, and target fit (slim, regular, relaxed, athletic) all shift the real-world measurements behind a labeled size. This calculator gives a reliable starting point based on your actual body measurements — treat it as a strong first estimate, and check a specific brand's own chart when a purchase really needs to fit precisely.

Dresses — Why the Larger Match Wins

Bust, waist, and hip rarely fall into exactly the same size range for every body. When they span two sizes, this calculator recommends the larger of the three matches, since a dress can usually be taken in at the smaller measurement points by a tailor, but sizing down and hoping for stretch is a much less reliable fix.

Getting an Accurate Shoe Measurement

Foot length is best measured standing (not sitting), from the heel to the tip of the longest toe, and ideally in the evening — feet swell slightly over the course of a day, so an evening measurement reflects real-world sizing more closely than a first-thing-in-the-morning one.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is a size calculator like this? It's a reliable starting point based on standard international size ranges, but brand cuts vary meaningfully — a size M in one brand's athletic fit can measure differently from a size M in a relaxed or suiting fit. Check the brand's own chart when precision matters.

Why does the dress size default to the largest matching measurement? When bust, waist, and hip measurements fall into different size ranges, sizing up to the largest match is generally more comfortable, since a dress can usually be taken in at the smaller points but not let out much at a smaller size.

How should I measure my foot for shoe sizing? Measure from the heel to the tip of the longest toe while standing, ideally in the evening when feet are slightly more swollen from the day — this gives a truer real-world size than measuring first thing in the morning.

Why do men's and women's bottoms use different size formats? Men's bottoms are conventionally sized by waist measurement in inches (like 32 or 34), while women's bottoms commonly use a numeric US size (like 6 or 8) that doesn't map directly to a single measurement — this calculator follows each convention separately.

Is this clothing size calculator free to use? Yes, it is completely free with no account or sign-up required, and runs instantly in your browser.

Does this store or upload my measurements? No — everything is calculated locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server.

Note: This tool provides general sizing guidance based on standard measurement ranges — always sanity-check against a brand's own size chart when it's available, especially for a purchase that needs to fit precisely.
