Free Calorie Calculator — Find Your Daily Calorie Needs

This calorie calculator estimates how many calories you burn each day and how many you should eat to lose, maintain, or gain weight. Enter your age, gender, height, weight, and activity level — the result appears instantly, right in your browser, with no sign-up required.

How Are Daily Calorie Needs Calculated?

This tool first estimates your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) — the calories your body burns at complete rest — using the Mifflin-St Jeor formula, one of the most accurate equations used by dietitians. It then multiplies your BMR by an activity factor based on how often you exercise, giving your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE), also called your maintenance calories.

Activity Level Guide

Sedentary means little to no exercise; lightly active is light exercise 1–3 days a week; moderately active is moderate exercise 3–5 days a week; very active is hard exercise 6–7 days a week; and extra active covers very hard daily training or a physically demanding job. Picking the closest match gives a more accurate calorie estimate.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many calories do I need per day? It depends on your age, gender, height, weight, and activity level. This calculator estimates your maintenance calories, then adjusts up or down for weight gain or loss goals.

How is daily calorie need calculated? This calculator first estimates your Basal Metabolic Rate using the Mifflin-St Jeor formula, then multiplies it by an activity factor based on how often you exercise.

How many calories should I eat to lose weight? A common approach is eating around 500 calories below your maintenance level, which typically results in about 0.5 kg (1 lb) of weight loss per week.

What is TDEE? TDEE stands for Total Daily Energy Expenditure — the total number of calories your body burns in a day, including exercise. It's the same as your maintenance calorie number.

Is this calorie calculator free? Yes, it is completely free with no sign-up required, and runs instantly in your browser.

Does this store or upload my data? No — the calculation runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server.
