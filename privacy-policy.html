Last updated: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cozyleaf ("we," "us," "the site") handles information when you use cozyleaf.in. We built Cozyleaf to be simple and respectful of your privacy — most tools process your data entirely in your browser and never send it anywhere.

Cozyleaf does not require an account, and most tools (calculators, image conversion, typing test) run entirely on your device. Files or numbers you enter into these tools are never uploaded to a server or stored by us.

Like most websites, our hosting provider and any analytics tools we use may automatically log standard technical information, such as your approximate location, browser type, device type, and pages visited. This is used only to understand site usage and is not linked to your identity.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

5. Third-party links

Cozyleaf may contain links to third-party sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.

6. Children's privacy

Cozyleaf does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
