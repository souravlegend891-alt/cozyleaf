EST to IST Time Difference

India is 10 hours and 30 minutes ahead of Eastern Standard Time (winter), or 9 hours and 30 minutes ahead of Eastern Daylight Time (summer). A 9:00 AM EST meeting, for example, falls at 7:30 PM IST the same evening in India — workable for both sides if kept to the morning US / evening India window.

Who Uses the EST to IST Conversion

This pairing is common between New York, Washington D.C., Toronto, and India — frequently used in finance and consulting where Wall Street or D.C. hours matter, for East Coast recruiters interviewing India-based candidates, and for the large Indian diaspora concentrated around New Jersey and New York staying in touch with family back home.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the time difference between EST and IST? IST is 10 hours and 30 minutes ahead of EST in winter, and 9 hours and 30 minutes ahead of EDT during US Daylight Saving Time.

What is 9 AM EST in IST? 9:00 AM EST (winter) is 7:30 PM IST the same day; during EDT (summer) it's 6:30 PM IST.

Is EST the same as Eastern Time? Eastern Time refers to the US East Coast zone that switches between EST (standard) and EDT (daylight) depending on the time of year; this tool handles both automatically.
