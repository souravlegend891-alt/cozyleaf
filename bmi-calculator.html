What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a simple screening measurement calculated from your height and weight. It's widely used by doctors and health organizations as a quick way to estimate whether a person's weight falls into a healthy range for their height.

How Is BMI Calculated?

In metric units, BMI equals your weight in kilograms divided by your height in meters, squared. In imperial units, it's your weight in pounds divided by your height in inches squared, multiplied by 703. This calculator handles the math for you automatically in either unit system.

Is BMI a Perfect Measurement?

BMI is a useful general indicator, but it doesn't directly measure body fat and doesn't account for factors like muscle mass, bone density, age, or sex. Athletes or very muscular people may show a higher BMI without carrying excess body fat. Use BMI as a starting point, not a diagnosis, and speak with a healthcare provider for a complete picture of your health.
