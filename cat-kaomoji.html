How to Copy and Paste Cat Kaomoji

Tap the Copy button under any cat face, then paste it into Discord, Instagram, WhatsApp, a text message or a bio. The whole face is copied at once, so no symbol gets lost. If a face shows as boxes on an older phone, pick a simpler one such as (=^･ω･^=) that uses common characters.

What Makes a Cat Kaomoji

Most cat kaomoji use the same few parts. Whiskers are drawn with = or ^ on both sides of the face, ears with ฅ, ᐠ or ≽, and the nose or mouth with ω, ᴥ or ェ in the middle. Change the eyes and the mood changes: ・ for calm, ♡ for love, T or ; for tears, ｀ and ´ for anger.

Where Cat Kaomoji Work Best

Because they are plain text, cat kaomoji paste into chat apps, captions, comments, usernames and plain-text fields where picture emoji sometimes do not. Some platforms count every symbol in a short bio or username limit, so shorter faces like (^ᆺ^) fit better.

Cat Kaomoji FAQ

What is a cat kaomoji?

A cat kaomoji is a Japanese-style text face that looks like a cat, usually with whiskers and ears made from symbols, such as (=^･ω･^=).

How do I copy a cat kaomoji?

Tap the Copy button under the face, then paste it wherever you can type text.

Which cat kaomoji is the cutest?

That is a matter of taste, but ฅ^•ﻌ•^ฅ, (^ᆺ^) and ≽^•⩊•^≼ are good places to start for a soft, friendly look.

Why does a cat kaomoji show as boxes?

A few faces use symbols from other scripts, such as Thai letters, that some older devices cannot display. Choose a simpler face from the same mood.

Is this free?

Yes. There is no account or sign-up, and copying happens in your browser.
