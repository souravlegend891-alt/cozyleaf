How This Dog Food Calculator Works

This calculator starts from your dog's resting energy requirement (RER) — the calories needed at complete rest — using the vet-standard formula RER = 70 × (weight in kg)0.75. That baseline is then multiplied by a factor for life stage, activity level and body condition score to estimate your dog's actual daily calorie needs, rather than relying on a generic weight-to-cups chart.

Why Life Stage and Activity Matter

A growing puppy or a pregnant/nursing dog needs far more calories per pound than a typical neutered adult, while a working dog or a highly active dog burns significantly more than one that's mostly sedentary. Selecting the right life stage and activity level keeps the daily target realistic instead of over- or under-feeding.

Reading Your Results

The results show a daily calorie target, the equivalent portion in cups, cans or grams based on your food's calorie density, a per-meal breakdown, and a treat allowance capped at 10% of daily calories — a common guideline vets use to avoid displacing balanced nutrition.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the daily calorie target calculated? It uses RER = 70 × (weight in kg)0.75, then multiplies by a factor for life stage, activity level and body condition score.

Is this free to use? Yes, completely free with no account required, and it runs entirely in your browser.

Can I use this for puppies? Yes — select the Puppy life stage for a higher growth-appropriate multiplier. For very young or toy-breed puppies, also check with your vet.

Does this replace advice from a vet? No. It's a starting estimate — dogs with medical conditions, extreme sizes, or a body condition score far from ideal should be assessed by a veterinarian.
