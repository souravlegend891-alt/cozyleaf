Frequently Asked Questions

Is my image stored anywhere?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser — your photo is never sent to a server, so it stays completely private.

Does this cost anything?

No, it's completely free and no account is required.

How accurate is the result?

The AI model generally handles hair, animals, and complex edges well, though results can be slightly less precise on very cluttered or poorly lit photos.

Why is the first run slower?

The first run needs to download the AI model, which takes a moment. After that, your browser caches it so future runs are much faster.
