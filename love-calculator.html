Free Love Calculator — Check Name Compatibility

This love calculator takes two names and turns them into a compatibility percentage, using a playful letter-and-numerology based formula. Type both names, tap Calculate, and see the overall score, a category-by-category breakdown, and — if you add both birth dates — a short zodiac compatibility note. Everything runs instantly in your browser, with no sign-up required.

How the Score Is Calculated

The calculator combines the letters of both names and counts how often each letter of the word "LOVES" appears. Those counts are then repeatedly paired and reduced down to two final digits, which become the compatibility percentage. It's the same idea behind the classic "FLAMES" and pen-and-paper love-calculator games many people played growing up — just automated. Because the formula only depends on the letters typed in, the same two names will always produce the exact same score every time.

What the Category Breakdown Shows

Beyond the overall percentage, the results break the score down into five playful categories — Communication, Trust, Passion, Fun & Humor, and Long-term Stability. Each category number is derived from the same two names using a related but distinct calculation, so the categories vary from each other while still connecting back to the overall score. Like the main score, these are for entertainment and don't reflect any real assessment of a relationship.

Understanding the Zodiac Bonus

Adding both birth dates unlocks an optional zodiac section. The calculator identifies each person's zodiac sign from their birth date, then shows a short note about how their elements — Fire, Earth, Air, or Water — traditionally pair in astrology. This is a fun addition layered on top of the main score, not a separate scientific calculation, and birth dates are entirely optional.

Is a Love Calculator Accurate?

No — and it isn't meant to be. Real compatibility depends on communication, shared values, effort, and countless factors a name alone could never capture. This tool is a lighthearted way to have fun with a friend, a partner, or a crush's name — not a serious relationship assessment. Use the "Copy result" button to quickly share a fun result, and "Try another pair" to test as many name combinations as you like.
