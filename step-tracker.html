Free Online Step Tracker — Live Pedometer in Your Browser

Timex Activity & Step Tracker counts your steps using your phone's built-in motion sensor while this page is open, or lets you log steps manually for any day. Set a daily goal, watch your progress ring fill up, track distance and estimated calories, and build a streak — all without an account, an app install, or any data leaving your device.

How does live step tracking work?

Modern phone browsers can read the device's accelerometer through the DeviceMotion API. This tracker watches the pattern of acceleration as you walk and counts each step using a peak-detection algorithm — the same basic idea most phone pedometer apps use. Tracking only runs while this tab is open and active, and it asks for motion-sensor permission first on iOS devices.

How are distance and calories estimated?

Distance is calculated as your step count multiplied by your stride length. If you don't set a stride length directly, it's estimated from your height using a standard walking-stride formula. Calories are a rough estimate based on your weight and the distance covered — useful as a general guide, not a precise medical measurement.

Streaks and achievements

Every day you meet your step goal extends your streak, shown at the top of the page. Badges unlock automatically as you hit milestones — your first logged day, single-day step counts, streak lengths, and lifetime totals — giving you a reason to come back and keep the habit going.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does live step tracking work in a browser? It uses your phone's motion sensor through the DeviceMotion API. While tracking runs and the page stays open, each detected step adds to today's total.

Is my step data uploaded anywhere? No — everything is stored only in your browser's local storage on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Can I log steps manually? Yes, use the Manual Log form to add or correct steps for any date.

How are distance and calories calculated? Distance uses your stride length; calories are estimated from your weight and distance walked. Treat both as approximations.

Is this step tracker free? Yes, completely free, no sign-up required, and it runs instantly in your browser.
