    Why Convert to JPG?

    JPG remains the most universally supported image format — every phone, website, printer, and document tool can open it without issue. If you have a screenshot in PNG, a webpage image in WebP, or an iPhone photo saved as HEIC, converting it to JPG makes it easy to share, upload, or print without compatibility problems.

    How the Conversion Works

    Each image is drawn onto an in-memory canvas at its original size, or scaled down if you set a maximum dimension, then re-encoded as JPG at the quality level you choose. Because JPG has no transparency channel, any transparent pixels are filled with your selected background color first. HEIC photos are decoded to a standard image format before this same process runs. Everything happens locally — your files are never sent to a server.

    Batch Conversion and Downloads

    You can add several images at once and convert them all together — the tool shows live progress as each one finishes. Download each JPG individually, tap a thumbnail to compare the original against the converted version before saving, copy a single result straight to your clipboard, or use "Download all (ZIP)" to get every converted file bundled into one archive.

    Fine-Tuning Your Output

    Beyond the quality slider, you can optionally target a specific file size in KB — useful when a website or form has an upload limit — and the converter automatically finds a quality level that gets close to it. You can also add a custom file name prefix, applied to every download and to the ZIP, which is handy for keeping a batch organized.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What image formats can I convert to JPG? You can convert PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, SVG, and HEIC (iPhone photo format) images to JPG.

    Does converting HEIC photos from my iPhone work? Yes. HEIC files are automatically decoded in your browser and converted to JPG, so iPhone photos work without any extra app.

    Are my images uploaded to a server? No. All conversion happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Your images never leave your device.

    Why does a background color option appear? JPG does not support transparency. If your source image has transparent areas, they are filled with the background color you choose before conversion.

    Can I convert multiple images at once? Yes. You can add several images together, convert them all in one click, and download them individually or as a single ZIP file.

    Does this reduce my image quality? You control the JPG quality with a slider from 1 to 100. Higher values preserve more detail but produce larger files; lower values shrink the file size.

    Can I target a specific file size, like under 200KB? Yes. Turn on the target file size option and enter a size in KB — the converter automatically adjusts the JPG quality to get as close as possible to that size.

    What happens with very large photos? Extremely large images are automatically scaled down slightly before conversion to keep the process fast and reliable on phones, instead of risking a crash or freeze.

    Is this converter free? Yes, it is completely free with no sign-up required, and runs instantly in your browser.

    Note: Very large images or a large batch may take a few extra seconds to process on older phones, since all conversion runs on your device rather than a server. Images are converted one at a time and automatically resized if needed, to keep memory use low and avoid crashes.
