Free World Time Converter — Any City, Any Time

Most time converters only handle one fixed pair of zones. This tool works the way the big international time-zone sites do — search for any city or country on either side, and it instantly shows live clocks and lets you convert any date and time between the two, anywhere in the world.

Built for Global Use

Whether you're in Europe scheduling a call with someone in Asia, in the US comparing notes with a colleague in Africa, or anywhere else entirely, this converter isn't limited to a handful of pre-set regions — it covers major cities across North and South America, Europe, Africa, the Middle East, South and East Asia, and Oceania, so anyone, anywhere, can look up their own comparison.

How It Handles Daylight Saving Time

Every conversion runs through your browser's built-in time zone database rather than a fixed offset table, so Daylight Saving Time changes are applied automatically and correctly for the exact date you pick — even for zones where DST starts and ends on different dates than your own.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I compare the time between any two cities in the world? Yes. Search for any city or country on either side and the converter instantly shows the live time and lets you convert any date and time between them.

Does this world time converter handle Daylight Saving Time? Yes. It uses your browser's built-in time zone database, so Daylight Saving Time transitions are applied automatically and correctly for the exact date you choose, in both directions.

What if my city isn't in the list? Search for the nearest major city in the same country — most places in a country share the same time zone, so a nearby major city will almost always give you the correct result.

Can I find the best meeting time between two zones? Yes. The Meeting Planner section below the converter shows a full 24-hour overlap view for the selected date, highlighting hours that fall within typical business hours (9 AM–6 PM) in both zones.

Is this world time converter free to use? Yes, it's completely free, works instantly in your browser, and does not require any sign-up.

Note: This converter uses your browser's built-in time zone database and automatically accounts for Daylight Saving Time changes for the selected date and cities, so results stay accurate year-round.
