IST to EST Time Difference

Eastern Standard Time is 10 hours and 30 minutes behind IST in winter, or 9 hours and 30 minutes behind during US Daylight Saving Time (EDT). A 7:00 PM IST call, for instance, falls at 8:30 AM EST the same day on the US East Coast — a workable evening-India / morning-US window.
